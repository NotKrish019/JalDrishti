# Jaldrishti Project Context & Philosophical Framework

**Project:** Jaldrishti (Jal-Dharohar Digital Intelligence & Conservation Platform)  
**Hackathon Focus:** Viksit Bharat / Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Digital Engineering Hackathon  
**Current Phase:** **PHASE 0 (Repository Bootstrap & Governance)**

---

## 1. The Core Problem
Traditional Indian water structures—including **Baolis, Bawaris, Kunds, Vavs (stepwells)**, and indigenous village percolation tanks—represent centuries of sophisticated hydraulic engineering, community water stewardship, and climate resilience.

Today, these irreplaceable heritage assets face grave challenges:
1. **Severe Physical Degradation:** Rampant vegetation root penetration, masonry cracking, siltation, structural spalling, and dislodgement.
2. **Incompatible Modern Interventions:** Inappropriate "restoration" using non-breathable Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) and polymer coatings that trap moisture, cause efflorescence, and accelerate the structural destruction of historic stone masonry.
3. **Fragmented & Disappearing Knowledge:** The indigenous craftsmanship rules, regional mortar formulations (lime-surkhi with fermented herbal additives), and aquifer recharge logic remain scattered across archaic texts, elderly artisans, and inaccessible archives.
4. **Lack of Digital Scalability:** No unified digital platform exists to assist conservation architects, archaeologists, and civic administrators in non-destructively scanning, assessing, and planning authentic conservation for thousands of stepwells across India.

## 2. The Core Philosophy

```
  [ SEE ]        ──►   Computer Vision (defect detection, instance segmentation, visual quantification)
  [ UNDERSTAND ] ──►   Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) & Historical Material DNA
  [ ASSESS ]     ──►   Deterministic Engineering Rules & Hydrological Viability Scoring
  [ REVIVE ]     ──►   Authentic, Material-Compatible Conservation & Restoration Planning
```

1. **SEE (Drishti):** Non-invasive visual inspection using instance segmentation to identify defects (cracks, roots, spalling) and measure relative defect coverage.
2. **UNDERSTAND (Jnana):** Contextualizing the structure through Indian Knowledge Systems—identifying regional typology, architectural intent, and authentic historical materials.
3. **ASSESS (Parikshan):** Quantitative engineering evaluation using deterministic, transparent rules to compute condition and functionality indices.
4. **REVIVE (Punarnirman):** Synthesizing heritage conservation principles with traditional material formulations to formulate actionable, safe restoration strategies.
