## 1. 10-Step End-to-End Live Demo Sequence

During the final hackathon presentation, Jaldrishti will demonstrate the full cycle of heritage preservation across 10 structured steps:

```
Step 1: Upload Field Survey Imagery
  └── The user uploads photographs of a heritage water structure (e.g. Darbhanga Fort kund or Delhi baoli).

Step 2: Select Inspection Region & Typology
  └── User selects structure typology (Baoli / Kund / Vav) and tags geographic region.

Step 3: Trigger Analysis
  └── User clicks "Analyse Heritage Structure", initiating the unified FastAPI backend pipeline.

Step 4: Real-Time Defect Detection (SEE)
  └── Computer vision overlays segmented cracks, vegetation intrusion, and spalling onto the interface.

Step 5: Quantify Visible Damage
  └── Displays precise image-space coverage percentages, connected component counts, and defect severities.

Step 6: Explain Indigenous Knowledge Principles (UNDERSTAND)
  └── System contextualizes traditional water catchment logic, historical aquifer connectivity, and heritage significance.

Step 7: Display Material Compatibility Matrix (ASSESS)
  └── Visualizes the 7-dimension compatibility radar comparing traditional lime-surkhi with destructive Portland cement.

Step 8: Generate Phased Restoration Roadmap (REVIVE)
  └── Outputs a structured 4-phase conservation intervention plan with authentic recipes and warnings.

Step 9: Interactive 3D Digital Twin Viewer
  └── Launches the Three.js viewport, allowing the judges to explore the 3D stepwell model with projected defect heatmaps.

Step 10: Before / After Conservation Simulation
  └── Interactive slider demonstrating simulated restoration: vegetation de-rooted, masonry repointed with hydraulic lime, and clean water revived.
```

---

## 2. Phase 0 Readiness Note
All 10 steps above represent **planned functionality** designed into the architecture during Phase 0. No fabricated mockups or hardcoded results are presented in Phase 0.
