# Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Hydrology Subsystem

**Owner:** Kirti Antil (IKS / Material Compatibility / Conservation Engineering Lead)

---

## 1. Scope & Objective
This directory articulates traditional Indian hydrological concepts and water stewardship ethics governing historic water structures (Baoli, Bawari, Kund, Vav).

The primary goal is to translate historical engineering wisdom into actionable, deterministic rules that the Jaldrishti digital platform can consume during condition assessment, root-cause diagnosis, and restoration planning.

---

## 2. Core Hydrological Tenets
1. **Dhal-Yojana (Gradient-Driven Gravity Flow):** Designing for natural topographic flow without external energy input.
2. **Bhugarbha Sheetalata (Subterranean Thermal Dampening):** Exploiting subterranean thermal inertia to suppress evaporation.
3. **Gadh-Niyantran (Multi-Stage Gravity Siltation Interception):** Trapping sediment before it enters the primary water reservoir.
4. **Jal-Samanvaya (Two-Way Aquifer Equilibrium):** Functioning as both recharge sink and dry-season withdrawal source.
5. **Pavitrata-Bhedana (Functional Hydraulic Zoning):** Physically separating pedestrian contact zones from potable abstraction shafts.
