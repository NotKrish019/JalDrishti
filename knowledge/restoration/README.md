# Traditional Restoration Principles & Minimum Intervention Ethics

**Owner:** Kirti Antil (IKS / Material Compatibility / Conservation Engineering Lead)

---

## 1. Conservation Philosophy
Jaldrishti operates under a strict conservation-first ethos aligned with ASI, INTACH, and UNESCO guidelines:
1. **Preserve Original Fabric:** Maximum retention of authentic stone masonry and historic lime bedding.
2. **Understand Root Cause:** Diagnose underlying causes (vegetation expansion, blocked drainage, groundwater depression) before treating surface symptoms.
3. **Minimum Intervention:** Intervene only to the extent necessary to ensure structural stability and water functionality.
4. **Material Compatibility:** Use materials whose physical and chemical characteristics match or are softer/more permeable than the original fabric.
5. **Reversibility:** Ensure repairs can be removed in the future without damaging the original historic substrate.
6. **Preserve Indigenous Wisdom:** Protect and record traditional craftsmanship knowledge and master-builder techniques.

---

## 2. Incompatible Practices Warning
- **Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC):** Non-breathable, excessively rigid, introduces soluble salts (sulfates/alkalis), and causes accelerated stone spalling.
- **Polymer / Synthetic Sealers:** Trap rising dampness inside stone cores, inducing cryptoflorescence and sub-surface fracture.
- **Indiscriminate Crack Infilling:** Filling structural movement joints without diagnosing active foundation settlement or hydrostatic thrust.
